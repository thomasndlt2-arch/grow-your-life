Every part of your life is a plant in your garden.

Let’s talk

Have a question, a problem or an idea for Grow Your Life? Write to info@gusela-solutions.it. For ordinary support requests, I usually reply within a few days. Include the app version and what happens; there’s no need to send your diary or other personal content.

Version 1.0

Visits, invitations, gifts, bees, the hive and visit safety tools are not active in version 1.0. They will return in an update. For support and data requests, including earlier tests, write to info@gusela-solutions.it.

Your subscription

To manage or cancel renewal: iPhone Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Grow Your Life. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel your subscription. For purchase or refund requests, see Apple’s refund support.

Your garden and your data

You can manage your private copy in Settings ▸ “Copy in iCloud”, recover your garden with “Find your garden again”, and delete data in Settings ▸ “Your data” ▸ “Delete all data”. iCloud deletions require a connection. The Privacy policy explains data, retention and rights. Subscriptions and promo codes on the subscription screen remain available; the invitation discount is unavailable in 1.0.

During setup, a neutral age-range question offers three equally presented answers, none selected in advance: “Under 16”, “16 or older”, “I'd rather not say”. Where available, we request Apple’s Declared Age Range signal only for the age threshold of 16. We do not ask for or store your date of birth. Your phone keeps only the age range and minimum information needed to apply and update protections, until corrected or deleted. We do not publish the age range, send it to analytics or include it in the garden copy. Apple’s signal is an age declaration, not a document-based identity check.

The purpose is to protect minors (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f)). In 1.0, age range controls access to optional analytics: below 16 or without a usable range, TelemetryDeck stays off and consent is not requested. From age 16, consent is still required. Your personal garden remains fully usable. If sources disagree, the more protective result applies. You can correct your answer in “Settings” ▸ “Privacy” ▸ “Your age”.
