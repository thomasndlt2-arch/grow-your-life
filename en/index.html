Support

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Every part of your life is a plant in your garden.

Let’s talk

Have a question, a problem or an idea for Grow Your Life? Write to info@gusela-solutions.it. For ordinary support requests, I usually reply within a few days. Include the app version and what happens; there’s no need to send your diary or other personal content.

Reporting and safety

In a garden you are visiting, open “…” and choose “Report the name” or “Report the garden”; for a gift in the hive, open its “…” and choose “Report”. For other problems, go to “Settings” ▸ “Visit safety” ▸ “Report a problem”. The same menus offer “Block this person”, with a confirmation; blocking is separate from reporting.

Choose a reason and tap “Prepare the email”. The app prepares an email to info@gusela-solutions.it; check it and press Send in your email app. For an identified name, garden or gift, the subject is “Report · Grow Your Life · GYL-R1 XXXXXXXX” and the body contains the full GYL-R1.… reference, which you should not change. “Report a problem”, without specific content, does not include that reference. The app neither sends automatically nor issues receipts: opening, closing, copying or saving a draft does not confirm sending or delivery.

Thomas reviews reports and replies within 24 hours. The Rules for visits and invitations explain what to include and how to ask for a review. “Block this person” is a separate choice: it hides that person’s interactions but does not send a report.

Your subscription

To manage or cancel renewal: iPhone Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Grow Your Life. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel your subscription. For purchase or refund requests, see Apple’s refund support.

Your garden and your data

Manage your private copy in Settings ▸ “Copy in iCloud” and delete data in Settings ▸ “Your data” ▸ “Delete all data”. Deletion from iCloud needs an internet connection. Our privacy policy explains what stays on your phone, what becomes public through visits and invitations, and how to exercise your rights.

Visits and invitations are available from age 13; optional analytics only from age 16 and with your consent. You can keep using your personal garden without these features. Received an invitation?

The age question offers four choices, with none selected in advance: “Under 13”, “13 to 15”, “16 or older”, “I'd rather not say”. Where available, Apple’s age range also applies, without a date of birth; if the sources disagree, the more protective range applies. With “I'd rather not say”, only what Apple confirms applies; without a usable range, visits, invitations and analytics remain off. Under 16, you are not asked for analytics consent. You can correct your answer in “Settings” ▸ “Privacy” ▸ “Your age”.
