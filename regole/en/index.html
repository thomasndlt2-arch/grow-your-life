Last updated: 15 October 2026

Visits and invitations are not active yet

Visits and invitations are not active in the app yet: they are unavailable in this version, along with gifts, bees, the hive, the invitation discount and visit safety tools. This page remains accessible for people with an old link.

Visits and invitations will return in an update after the planned protective measures are completed. Before reactivation, we will publish the applicable rules and update the privacy policy. In this version, there are no rules to accept for these features.

You can always write to info@gusela-solutions.it for support, data rights or issues from earlier tests. See Support and the Privacy policy for the applicable channels and response times.
